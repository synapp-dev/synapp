How to read this document

Each requested feature is assessed against the current codebase and given a build status, an effort band and an indicative price. Items are grouped by the client’s change-request headings. Every section and every item begins on its own page.

Basis of estimate
Pricing uses a day rate derived from the historical delivery benchmark for this platform: approximately $250,000 AUD for ~4 months of full-time delivery of the existing Bullyproof codebase (~86 working days), i.e. ≈ $2,900 AUD per working day. Item estimates are engineering effort only and exclude design iteration, content authoring, UAT and project management unless noted. Ranges are indicative for scoping, not a fixed quote.
